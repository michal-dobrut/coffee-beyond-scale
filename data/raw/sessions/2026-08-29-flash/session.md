# session "flash"
in order of shooting

a probe, not a campaign: two frames asking whether the phone's integrated
flash is worth using at all. Same coffee, same substrate and same balance as
the morning's "run zero", seven hours later and under a different light, which
is why it is its own session rather than a stage of that one.

raw only, no jpeg sidecar.

scale: kitchen scale, resolution = 1 g
weighted in a cup (cup is 67g), spreaded on paper by hand
lighting: integrated phone flash, forced on (EXIF flash = 9, "fired, compulsory")
coffee: espresso-roast, "Brazylia Monte Carmelo", variety arabica "mundo nuovo, paraiso", preparation "natural", roasted 2026-08-10, roastery: "Kafar"
